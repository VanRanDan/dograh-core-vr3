## About VR3 AI

**VR3 AI is VanRan's internal voice agent platform** — built on top of the open-source [dograh](https://github.com/dograh-hq/dograh) project and customized for building, deploying, and maintaining customer-facing voice agents.

The platform is self-hosted on VanRan's infrastructure and is accessible to authorized team members only.

## Accessing the Platform

VR3 AI is deployed at **[vr3ai.tapcloud.org](https://vr3ai.tapcloud.org)** (production) and **[192.11.0.36](https://192.11.0.36)** (QA/staging).

<Note>
  Accounts are provisioned by a platform administrator. There is no public sign-up. Contact your administrator to get access.
</Note>

### Logging In

1. Navigate to your environment's URL
2. Enter your email and password
3. If this is your first login, you will be prompted to set a new password before continuing

### Switching Organizations

If your account belongs to multiple organizations, you can switch between them using the org selector in the top-left of the sidebar. The page reloads automatically to reflect the selected organization's resources.

## What You Can Build

- **Voice Agents** — drag-and-drop workflow builder for inbound and outbound voice AI
- **Campaigns** — automated outbound call campaigns to contact lists
- **Knowledge Bases** — attach documents for retrieval-augmented generation
- **Telephony Integrations** — connect Twilio, Telnyx, Bandwidth, and other providers
